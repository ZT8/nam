Help

Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Same day within Dubai for orders before 4 pm, and 24 to 48 hours across the rest of the UAE. Delivery is free nationwide.

Can I exchange or return?

Sleepwear can be exchanged within 7 days in its original condition with tags. Parfum and powder cannot be returned once opened.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery, or card and Apple Pay through a payment link we send on WhatsApp after confirming your order.

How do I choose my size?

Our cuts are relaxed. If you are between sizes, choose the smaller one. We can send the size chart in centimetres on WhatsApp.
